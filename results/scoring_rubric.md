# Scoring Rubric for Assignment 2 Evaluation

Each answer is scored from 1 to 5 against the assignment criteria. A score of 5 means the response is strong, reliable, and useful for the intended beginner user. A score of 1 means the response fails that criterion.

## 1. Correctness
- **5**: The answer is factually accurate with respect to the relevant Shakespeare play(s), includes the main expected points, and contains no misleading claims.
- **3**: The answer is partly correct but misses an important point, is too vague, or includes a minor unsupported claim.
- **1**: The answer is mostly incorrect, confuses characters/events/plays, or hallucinates major details.

## 2. Grounding
- **5**: The answer clearly uses the retrieved evidence and does not go beyond what the evidence can support.
- **3**: The answer is generally related to the evidence but also relies on unsupported background knowledge or weakly connects to the passages.
- **1**: The answer is not supported by the retrieved evidence or ignores the evidence entirely.

## 3. Retrieval Relevance
- **5**: Retrieved passages are directly relevant and include useful metadata such as play, act, scene, speaker, and excerpt.
- **3**: Some retrieved passages are relevant, but others are only loosely connected or too broad.
- **1**: Retrieved passages are irrelevant, from the wrong play/context, or do not help answer the question.

## 4. Usefulness for a Beginner
- **5**: The answer is clear, concise, beginner-friendly, and explains motivations, relationships, or old language where needed.
- **3**: The answer is understandable but lacks explanation, assumes some prior knowledge, or is not focused enough.
- **1**: The answer is confusing, overly technical/literary, too brief, or not helpful for someone new to Shakespeare.

## 5. Style Quality
Style is scored mainly for stylised-generation questions. For non-stylised questions, assign **N/A** or leave blank in the detailed table.
- **5**: The response has a recognisable Shakespearean tone, remains understandable, is clearly labelled as creative, and stays under 150 words.
- **3**: The response has some Shakespearean features but is uneven, slightly unclear, weakly labelled, or close to the word limit.
- **1**: The response is not recognisably stylised, is hard to understand, exceeds 150 words, or presents creative writing as factual evidence.
